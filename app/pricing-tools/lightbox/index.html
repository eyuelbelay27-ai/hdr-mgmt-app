Auto-calc: when checked, the Material Cost tab asks for an m² value for this item and computes its quantity automatically (m² × Multiplier). Area Source decides whether that m² is auto-filled from the Lightbox tab's Signage Area or its Mica Area. The "led module" and "Relief" items use their own dedicated formulas instead of a plain Multiplier — edit those in "Formula Settings" below. Min/Max Qty are optional limits — on the Material Cost tab, entering a value below Min automatically fills Min, and above Max automatically fills Max, shortly after typing stops (or immediately on leaving the field). Default Value: whenever this item's Qty (or m² for auto-calc items) is empty, it's pre-filled with this number on the Material Cost tab — still fully editable per job. Leave blank for no default.

Formula Settings

These formulas drive the automatic calculations across the app. Every range and constant below is editable — nothing here is hardcoded. Ranges are min-inclusive / max-exclusive (leave Max blank for "no upper limit" — the highest range automatically covers everything above its Min).

Mica Formula (by Signage Area)

Mica Area = Signage Area × the multiplier for whichever range the Signage Area falls into. Editing any value here instantly recalculates the current quote's Mica-flagged items.

LED Module Formula (by Signage Area)

LED Module quantity = Signage Area × the multiplier for whichever range the Signage Area falls into. Applies automatically to the "led module" catalog item — no manual qty entry needed.

Relief Formula (by Mica Area)

Relief quantity = (Multiplier × Mica Area) + Constant. Applies automatically to the "Relief" catalog item — no manual qty entry needed.

Metal Formula (by Signage Width & Height)

Metal (linear meters) = (2 × Width) + (2 × Height) + (MAX(0, FLOOR(Width) − 1) × Height) — outer frame plus one internal vertical support per full meter of width. That result is then converted to "berga" (rounded UP to the nearest whole berga) using the conversion below. Applies automatically to the "metal" catalog item, driven by the Signage Width/Height on the Lightbox Quote tab — no manual qty entry needed.

"Include in This Quote" list

Choose which items appear in the "Include in This Quote" list on the Lightbox Quote tab, and which of them start ticked on a new quote. Items that start unticked stay out of Material Cost until someone ticks them (or adds them with "Add Item"). Quick-Fill items have their own section above.

Conditional Rules

Set a rule so one item's value automatically fills another — e.g. "if Signage Area ≥ 10, set Footing to 3". The condition checks the trigger item's current quantity (m² × multiplier for auto-calc items) on the Material Cost tab; whenever it's met, the target item is filled with the given value.
